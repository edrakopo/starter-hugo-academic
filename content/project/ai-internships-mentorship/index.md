# Mentorship & Supervision: AI-Driven Reconstruction & Event Selection
---
Since 2016, supervised 20+ undergraduate, MSc and PhD students and research assistants applying machine and deep learning to neutrino event reconstruction, event classification, explainability and uncertainty estimation across KM3NeT and ANNIE. Several supervisions led to formal thesis research (see KM3NeT/ANNIE) or conference contributions (Neutrino2024, ICRC2025, EuCAIFCon 2026).

**Selected highlights:**

**Efthymios Grizanitis (intern, 2026)** - employed DynEdge ([GraphNeT](https://github.com/graphnet-team/graphnet)), a Graph Neural Network optimised for large neutrino telescopes, for vertex and direction reconstruction of the incident neutrino in small water Cherenkov detectors (ANNIE). 

**Melina Lazarou** - from summer intern to BSc thesis on deposited energy reconstruction using Graph Neural Networks (DynEdge ([GraphNeT](https://github.com/graphnet-team/graphnet))) for KM3NeT/ARCA; results presented at EuCAIFCon 2026.

**Chrysovalantis Karagiannis** - from summer intern in ANNIE (2022) to Affiliated Researcher in 2023, followed by an MSc thesis (2024) and supervised PhD candidate (2026–present), studying diffuse astrophysical neutrino flux with KM3NeT/ARCA.

**Panagiotis Gkotsis (BSc, 2023–2024)** - started as an intern in acoustic neutrino detection studies in the astroparticle physics group (2023) and progressed to a BSc thesis benchmarking DynEdge ([GraphNeT](https://github.com/graphnet-team/graphnet)) Graph Neural Network against [OrcaNet](https://github.com/KM3NeT/OrcaNet) for KM3NeT/ARCA energy reconstruction; results presented at [NEUTRINO2024](https://agenda.infn.it/event/37867/contributions/229767/attachments/121592/177349/Poster_Neutrino2024_KM3NeT_ARCA_GNN.pdf).

**Paraskevi Sakkou (BSc, 2022–2023)** - from summer intern to BSc thesis developing a vertex reconstruction method for water Cherenkov detectors employing Deep Learning Neural Networks, significantly improving reconstruction efficiency compared to maximum likelihood-based methods.

**Nikolaos Karafyllis (intern, 2023)** - comparative study of uncertainty estimation methods for ML-based energy predictions in ANNIE.
